# Closure of the 8 mixed-sector J bivectors — so(4) ⊕ so(4) (2026-10-26)

Record of the triangulation computation in the "Map physics notations
to model" chat, 2026-10-26 (Kit's index-candidate survey). Computed
exactly in-chat: independent Cl(4,4) build over ℚ(i), conventions
cross-validated by reproducing all 8 J² = −I₁₆.

## Result

The 8 mixed-sector J bivectors (E0E4, E0E6, E2E4, E2E6 / E1E5, E1E7,
E3E5, E3E7) generate a **12-dimensional** Lie algebra: the span of the
8 mixed bivectors plus exactly 4 more {E0E2, E1E3, E4E6, E5E7}.
Sample: [J0, J1] = −2E4E6 — commutators land in within-sector
bivectors, as the survey predicted. The derived algebra is the full
12 (perfect).

Identification by the leg split: the bivectors on {0,2,4,6} (all
E² = +1) and on {1,3,5,7} (all E² = −1) form two commuting closed
6-dimensional factors, so the generated algebra is
**so(4) ⊕ so(4)** (compact; su(2)⁴ at algebra level). Confirmed the
same day by the Killing-signature computation: both 6-dim factors
closed and mutually commuting, Killing form negative-definite with
all 12 eigenvalues exactly −16.

## Consequence

By dimension + simplicity, the algebra contains **no su(3)
subalgebra** (any projection of the simple su(3) into a 6-dim factor
is trivial). The triangulation therefore closes on a clean negative
for the J material as gluon indices, and identifies its actual
closure as compact SU(2)-type blocks.
