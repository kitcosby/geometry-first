# so(12,4): the third 120, and its nilpotent quarter

**Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Status: PROVED (exact) for the construction and
quarter; the sl(11) extraction is FALSIFIED in its containment forms.**
Local note, built at Kit's order. Context: dimension 120 is shared by
sl(11, ℝ) (FW ladder), compact so(16) (path ladder), and so(12,4).
Question posed: can an sl(11) be gotten out of so(12,4) — "a so(16)
with a nilpotent quarter"?

## 1. Construction (vector representation, exact)

η = diag(+1¹², −1⁴). Generators: E_{ab} − E_{ba} on same-sign pairs
(72 rotations), E_{ab} + E_{ba} on mixed pairs (48 boosts); 120 total.
Verified exactly (integer arithmetic): every generator satisfies
ηX + Xᵀη = 0; span rank 120 (mod p); all 7,140 brackets again satisfy
the defining condition (0 violations), so the span is the Lie algebra
so(12,4). **Killing signature (exact rational LDL): (48, 72, 0)** —
positive on the 48 boosts, negative on the 72 rotations.

The three 120s are thereby separated: sl(11, ℝ) (65, 55), so(16)
compact (0, 120), so(12,4) (48, 72) — pairwise non-isomorphic.

## 2. The nilpotent quarter (PROVED)

so(12,4) has Witt index 4. Explicit isotropic 4-plane (a quarter of
the 16): n_k = e_{8+k} + f_k, k = 1..4 (e's +directions, f's
−directions). Clifford side, with γ's built by the Pauli recursion and
the i-flip on the four negative directions: {γ_a, γ_b} = 2η_{ab} I
verified with max deviation exactly 0.0, and **γ(v)² = 0 for every v
in the quarter** (all 16 basis combinations checked) — γ restricted to
the quarter is nilpotent, since γ(v)² = Q(v)·I and Q ≡ 0 there.
Compact so(16) (Witt index 0) has no such quarter.

The stabilizer of the quarter (Witt basis, flag 0 ⊂ N ⊂ N^⊥ ⊂ V):
**parabolic of dimension 82** = Levi gl(4) ⊕ so(8) (16 + 28) plus a
**nilpotent radical of dimension 38** (= 4⊗8 ⊕ Λ²4 = 32 + 6),
computed over GF(p) at two primes (1000033, 999983, agreeing).
Radical elements obey X: V → N^⊥, N^⊥ → N, N → 0, hence X³ = 0.

## 3. Verdict on "an sl(11) out of so(12,4)"

- Isomorphism: NO — A₁₀ vs D₈; Killing signatures differ.
- Subalgebra: NO — equal dimensions force a 120-dim subalgebra to be
  the whole algebra, i.e. an isomorphism.
- Quotient: NO — so(12,4) is simple; its only quotients are 0 and
  itself.
- Not closed: a *contraction* (limit of structure constants) is none
  of the above and is not pursued; it is also not a containment claim.

The quarter is the real content: the (12,4) form is the one 120 of
the three carrying a large nilpotent organ (38 of its 120 dimensions
in the radical) anchored on an isotropic quarter of the space.
