# The Wall — Where Derivation Stops

**Program:** Start from the proven base (27 theorems). Derive with exact computer algebra. Mark precisely where the mathematics can go no further without additional hypotheses.

## What the base gives (PROVED)

1. **An irreducible 16.** Cl(4,4) acts irreducibly on ℝ¹⁶ (T1). No submodules. No invariant decomposition.
2. **A 16-dim bridge.** The intertwiner space V=Hom(R,J) is 16-dimensional (T2), a vector space (not an algebra, D2), with orthogonal geometry (Gram=4I).
3. **A mirror, not a rotation.** An involution X²=+I exists in V (T3). No REAL X²=−I exists in V (T4*: 2 complex solutions, 0 real). The 16 reflects; it does not rotate.
4. **Rich operator geometry.** V₄ symmetry, Möbius branch structure, conic hierarchy (hyperbola→ellipse→Veronese), exact dynamics (Bernoulli). All intrinsic, all derived.
5. **Natural 8+8.** Two invariant symmetric bilinear forms, both rank 8 (D3). The 16 carries an 8+8 in its geometry, unasked.

## Where it stops (THE WALL)

The base **does not** give:
- **A complex structure.** No J²=−I on ℝ¹⁶ compatible with the intertwiner structure (T4*). To get ℂ⁸, something must be added.
- **SU(8).** Requires ℂ⁸. Blocked at the complex-structure gap.
- **Fermion quantum numbers.** 10⊕5̄⊕1, (SU(3),SU(2),Y) assignments — nowhere in the base.
- **A physical projection.** Γ (units, scale, spacetime embedding) is not derived; the phase-to-radius map is a coordinate choice, not a theorem.
- **Dynamics of coupling.** g(x,i) is not in the base.

## What would cross the wall (HYPOTHESES, not in this repo)

- **Axiom Zero (A0.3):** posits an isomorphic imaginary sector. Would give doubling, but the doubling is already in Cl(4,4) algebraically — the axiom's added content is unclear.
- **8 Z's proposal:** Z_{i,M}=c_i+i·c_i' from RUN144 types. ASSERTED, not proven. Needs: explicit c_i', ℂ-independence, SU(8) invariance.
- **E8(−24):** a "representation host." Inspired by beauty (Lisi's "what if beauty is truth?"), not derived. Real-form mismatch: E8's SO(16) is compact; Cl(4,4) gives split SO(4,4).
- **Standard Model anchor:** using the SM as calibration target. Methodologically sound as a *target*, but not a derivation.

## The honest boundary

Everything on the near side is PROVED and in this repository. Everything on the far side is HYPOTHESIS and excluded. The wall is not a failure — it is the precise location where mathematics ends and choice begins. Any future work that adds a hypothesis must label it, locate it at the wall, and never smuggle it into the proofs.
