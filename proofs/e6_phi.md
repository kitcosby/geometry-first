# The Φ bridge: the two models of the 27 are the same representation

`e6_phi.py` — **27/27 exact checks over ℚ(i), exit 0.** This closes
the bridge between the two constructions of 𝔢₆'s 27-dimensional
representation built in this series:

- the **seed/rep model** (`e6_seed.py`, `e6_rep27.py`): 𝔢₆ =
  𝔰𝔬(10) ⊕ ℂZ₆ ⊕ 𝔭⁺ ⊕ 𝔭⁻ acting on V₂₇ = S ⊕ V ⊕ 1
  (16 ⊕ 10 ⊕ 1) built from the Spin(10) spinor;
- the **Jordan model** (`e6_jordan.py`, `e6_jordan_e6.py`):
  J = H₃(𝕆_ℂ) with its determinant, and 𝔢₆ realized intrinsically
  as the determinant-preserving algebra on J, graded by the Peirce
  frame E = diag(1, 0, 0) into pieces of dimensions 1, 16, 10.

**Theorem (computational, exact).** There exist a linear isomorphism
Φ : V₂₇ → J and a Lie algebra isomorphism α : 𝔢₆(seed) → 𝔢₆(Jordan)
such that

- Φ ρ_R(g) = ρ_J(α(g)) Φ for **all 78 generators g** (V1);
- α preserves **all 3,003 bracket pairs** (V2);
- det ∘ Φ = **16i · C** as cubic forms (V3, all 3,654 triples),
  where C is the rep model's invariant cubic (in the corrected
  normalization of the erratum below);
- Φ has rank 27 (V4).

So the Jordan determinant *is* the rep model's invariant cubic,
transported by Φ, and the two 𝔢₆'s are one algebra in two coordinate
systems. Everything is exact rational arithmetic over ℚ(i); no
floating point anywhere.

## How Φ and α are built (all steps exact solves, no ansatz fitting)

1. **Sides rebuilt** (P1): the Jordan algebra, its det tensor
   (89 entries), the det-preserving algebra solved sectorwise by
   Peirce charge — dims (46, 16, 16, 0, 0), total 78 — the Levi
   centre Ẑ normalized to Peirce block scalars (4, 1, −2), its
   derived 45; and on the rep side the 78 assembled operators of
   `e6_rep27.py`, rebuilt independently.
2. **The h-trick** (P2–P3): a generic torus element
   h_R = Σ a_k Y_(2k,2k+1), a = (1, 2, 4, 8, −15), is diagonal in the
   Fock basis on S with 16 distinct eigenvalues (the ±15 collision
   pair has opposite chirality, so no collision inside the sector).
   On the Jordan side the Peirce-frame torus K₀..K₄ is solved inside
   the derived Levi by prescribing its J₀ block (boost + four plane
   rotations); h_J is assembled with the same weights and its
   spinor spectrum is matched by **characteristic polynomials**.
   The match requires the **parity flip a₄ → +15** on the Jordan
   side (the two models' fifth plane orientations disagree; with
   the flip the spectra agree exactly). Φ₀ is then forced: V-piece
   by eigenvector pairing at equal h-eigenvalues (flip-adjusted),
   S-piece by 1-dimensional h_J-eigenspaces, singlet ↦ E.
   P3: Φ₀ h_R = h_J Φ₀ as operators.
3. **β₀ = α on the Levi** (P4): the centre maps Z′ ↦ Ẑ (weights
   agree on all 27 weight vectors). The plane torus images BK_k are
   solved from the 27 weight equations; their Ẑ-components come out
   **exactly zero**, as they must (𝔰𝔬(10) is perfect). The 40 root
   vectors of 𝔰𝔬(10) w.r.t. the plane torus are found on the rep
   side by an ansatz search over the four Σ's mixing two planes
   (the fourth coefficient is *real* ±1, the middle two ±i); each
   root space on the Jordan side is then solved in DERJ
   coefficients and is **exactly 1-dimensional**. The root scale
   factors t_g are propagated from the bracket relations (opposite
   -root products and root-sum relations); all 780 root–root
   relations then hold as full DERJ-coordinate vectors (P4h).
4. **Weight scales τ** (P5): within each piece of the 27, the
   ratios τ_w are propagated over the root-action graph (16 spinor
   weights and 10 vector weights, both connected and
   cycle-consistent), leaving one free scale per piece.
5. **The 𝔭 stage** (P6): α(T_{x₀}) for the vacuum x₀ = 0 is solved
   from the singlet- and S-input intertwining equations in one joint
   nullspace (18 unknowns: 16 operator coefficients + the two scale
   ratios); α(T_{y₀}) from the S-input equations, its scale locked
   to the x-stage through σ₁, with the V-input intertwining then
   **verified** on all 10 inputs. The remaining 𝔭 generators are
   generated by Levi brackets (α(T_x) = s⁻¹[β₀(Y_j), α(T_{x′})]
   along the monomial action graph). Resulting scales (in the gauge
   σ_S = 1): **σ₁/σ_S = −i, σ_V/σ_S = −4i**.

The pair (Φ, α) is a representative: the remaining freedoms (overall
Φ scale, the root-character/torus-conjugation freedom seeded at
t = 1 where the relations leave it open, and the x-stage nullspace
representative) are gauges — V1–V4 verify the resulting pair itself,
not a class.

## Erratum to `e6_rep27.py` (found in this build)

The rep27 note reported the invariant cubic as C ∝ 2·C₁ − C₂,
verified by a tensor-delta contraction. **That verification was
defective, and the (2, −1) ratio is superseded.** The tensor delta
in `e6_rep27.py` contracts a stored triple's three slots into a
single sorted bucket; on triples with a repeated index this counts
the derivation action with the wrong multiplicity (the two equal
slots are treated as two independent derivations into the same
bucket). Under the true derivation action
(δT)(x, y, z) = T(gx, y, z) + T(x, gy, z) + T(x, y, gz), the
(2, −1) tensor fails on exactly 10 triples per 𝔭 generator, and the
invariant ratio is **(1, −1)**:

C(U) = W(ψ, ψ) · v − s · (v · v),

re-verified in this build from scratch: derivation-δ = 0 under all
78 generators, and det ∘ Φ = 16i · C on all 3,654 triples. (An
earlier rep27 pass with the transposed contraction had in fact
produced (1, −1); it was set aside as "buggy" when the contraction
was changed. The bracket verifications, irreducibility, charges,
and the products a₁a₄ = −1/8, a₂a₃ = −1/16 of `e6_rep27.py` do not
depend on the tensor delta and stand as recorded.)

## Disclosures (machinery defects during this build; all fixed, math unaffected)

- `bracket_coords` initially dropped the structure constant from
  each summand (added `sgn·v_g·w_h` instead of `sgn·v_g·w_h·c`).
  Caught by a direct matrix-commutator cross-check (centralizer
  dimensions 29 = 28 + 1 confirmed the BK side was sound).
- The root ansatz first forced the fourth Σ-coefficient imaginary;
  it is real (±1). With that, exactly 40 roots are found.
- The x-stage singlet equation first placed the scale λ on the
  wrong side (the solve returned σ₁⁻¹ in place of σ₁); diagnosed
  from the exact factor λ² = −1 by which the [T_{x₀}, T_{y₀}]
  bracket check failed, and fixed by solving for μ = σ_S/σ₁.
- Two type slips: `pscale` (pair × Fraction) called with a pair
  second argument in three places (now `pmul`); the first V3 used
  the basis-index `det3J` on vectors (replaced by the polarized
  `det3v`), and the first V4 compared the (rank, pivots) tuple
  against 27.

## Scope

Complex Lie algebras and representations only: 𝔢₆ over ℂ, J over
ℂ, exact arithmetic over ℚ(i). This identifies the two complex
models. **Not** addressed here: real forms of either model, and the
EIII homogeneous geometry E₆/(Spin(10) × U(1)) (whose isotropy data
— the 62-dimensional maximal parabolic with 46-dimensional Levi —
is already in place from `e6_jordan_e6.py`).
